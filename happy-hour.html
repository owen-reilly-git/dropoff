Wednesday night trivia

Trivia Wed
Starts
8pm sharp
Sign-up
From 7:30pm at the bar
Teams
Up to 6 people
Cost
Free

How it works

  1. Pick a team name and grab an answer sheet from the bar.
  2. Play five rounds of ten questions: general knowledge, music, pictures, the neighborhood, and a wildcard.
  3. Hand in your sheet after each round. Scores go up on the board.
  4. The final round is double points, so nobody's out until the end.

Prizes

  • First place: $50 bar tab
  • Second place: a round of shots for the team
  • Best team name: a free pitcher
  • Last place: the lint trap, our trophy, until next week
